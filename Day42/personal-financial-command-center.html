Page 09 — Back Cover

Tips, Checklist & Resources

Habits worth building this term, a checklist you can actually finish, and prompts to keep learning.

Pay yourself first

Move savings out the moment income lands, before it has a chance to become spending.

Track subscriptions monthly

A ₹119 app adds up to ₹1,428 a year — worth a five-minute audit each month.

Start small, start now

₹500 a month at 20 beats ₹2,000 a month started at 30 — time matters more than amount.

Separate spending and saving

A second account (even a basic one) makes saved money harder to accidentally spend.

Build the cushion first

Even ₹5,000 set aside can absorb a surprise expense without derailing your month.

Review, don't restrict

A monthly 10-minute check-in beats a rigid budget you'll abandon by week two.

Term checklist

Check items off — your progress is saved automatically.
