Quarterly Growth Strategy Meeting

Transcript breakdown — Action Plan Dashboard

📋 Summary

The leadership team reviewed Q2 performance: revenue grew 12%, below the 18% annual target, largely due to enterprise deals slipping (9 of 14 expected contracts closed) from procurement delays and added security reviews. Marketing reported a 34% increase in website traffic but weaker conversion rates, prompting a planned website redesign. Engineering capacity is tight due to the August analytics dashboard release, raising concerns about taking on additional website work without a cost estimate. Customer retention is strong at 94%, but support tickets are rising due to reporting performance and dashboard loading time issues, which the team agreed is a priority. Hiring decisions, the conference budget, and the analytics dashboard announcement were all deferred pending further information.

💡 Key Takeaways

Q2 revenue grew 12%, below the 18% annual target.

Only 9 of 14 expected enterprise contracts closed; procurement delays and added security reviews cited as causes.

Website traffic increased 34% quarter-over-quarter, but conversion rates remain lower than expected.

Engineering is near capacity due to the August analytics dashboard release; added website work may require contractors.

Customer retention is strong at 94%, but support ticket volume has increased significantly.

Reporting performance and dashboard loading times identified as the main driver of support complaints and a sales objection point.
