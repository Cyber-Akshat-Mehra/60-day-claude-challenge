Day 4Comment on 5 posts in your target space

Value Comment formula: Agree with a specific point → Add one insight from your own experience (e.g. from building the Financial Analyzer) → Ask a genuine question. This gets you seen by the poster's audience without looking like spam.

Day 5Publish Post #2 — lessons-learned / hot take
Building a Financial Analyzer taught me something I didn't expect: The hardest part wasn't the React frontend or the Node/Express APIs. It was deciding what NOT to show the user. Live Nifty/Sensex data, budget planning, AI suggestions, transaction logs — it's easy to cram all of it onto one screen. It's much harder to make it feel simple. Hot take: most "full-stack" tutorials teach you to build features. Almost none teach you to cut them. What's a feature you built and then deleted?
~560 characters.
Day 6Engage + 5 more connection requests

Reply to every comment on both posts (even a one-liner keeps the algorithm pushing your post further). Send 5 more targeted requests using the Day 3 template.

Day 7Review profile views & connection acceptance rate

What the numbers mean: A jump in profile views after Day 1-2 = your headline/keywords are working. A low connection-acceptance rate (under ~30%) = your requests need to be more specific to the person, not generic. High post views but low comments = your hook is fine but your CTA/question isn't landing — try a more direct question next time.
