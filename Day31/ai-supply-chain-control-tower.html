📘 How to Run the Tower

  • Alerts arrive continuously — port congestion, supplier delays, breakdowns, and more.
  • Each alert has a priority and a countdown. If it expires, it auto-resolves badly.
  • Pick an action for each alert. Some actions are smart fixes, others are costly or lazy shortcuts.
  • Good decisions raise Service Level, Satisfaction, Inventory Health, Transport Efficiency, and Revenue Protected — while controlling cost.
  • Bad decisions (or ignoring critical issues) hurt KPIs and can trigger delayed follow-on consequences.
  • Survive 3 minutes. Alert frequency increases over time — stay sharp.
